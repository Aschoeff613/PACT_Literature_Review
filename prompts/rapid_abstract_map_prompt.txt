You are mapping ONE included title and abstract to the final PACT taxonomy of 17
clinician cognitive tasks. This is a rapid targeted review, not a systematic review.

Use only the supplied title, abstract, and taxonomy. Identify what the paper itself
examines. Do not infer a cognitive task merely because a clinician makes a medical
decision in the background.

Choose one to three taxonomy task IDs only when the abstract directly supports them.
The clinical_task is the concrete care activity (for example handoff, selecting an
antibiotic, deciding admission, or arranging follow-up). The cognitive tasks are the
thinking or communication processes that the paper actually studies.

The evidence_quote must be copied exactly from the supplied abstract. If no exact
supporting sentence exists, leave it empty and set pdf_needed to true.

Set pdf_needed to true only when:
- the abstract is too vague to map confidently;
- the paper may describe cognitive work outside the 17-task taxonomy; or
- the paper is particularly important but the supporting detail is only likely to
  appear in the full text.

Do not request a PDF merely because a full text exists. Most papers should not need one.
