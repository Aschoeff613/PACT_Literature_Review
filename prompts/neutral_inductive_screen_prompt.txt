You are screening a bounded, targeted evidence scan about HIGH-RISK COGNITIVE WORK
in adult emergency medicine and primary care. Its purpose is to discover a literature-
derived task taxonomy inductively. Do not assume any pre-existing task taxonomy.

For each paper, ask whether it provides a candidate cognitive task for open coding.
High-risk situations alone are NOT enough.

TRACK A — an explicit clinician cognitive action in a concrete situation. Examples
include noticing a signal, updating an explanation, interpreting conflicting evidence,
making a decision with incomplete information, communicating uncertainty, deciding
whether to admit/discharge, reconciling medication, or arranging follow-up. Describe
the action precisely; never use a bare label such as "clinical reasoning."

TRACK C — the action occurs in a specific high-risk clinical situation or workflow.
Plausible safety relevance is enough; a quantified error or harm rate is welcome but
not required.

IN SCOPE: adult emergency medicine; adult primary care; and admission/discharge or
care-transition decisions connecting those settings. Medication review and handoff
work count. Care delivered after a patient is established on an inpatient ward does
not count unless the paper concerns the admission or discharge decision/transition.

DO NOT INFER an action merely because a study evaluates a drug, an intervention, a
risk score, an AI system, a quality metric, or a clinical outcome. The title or
abstract must explicitly describe, analyse, measure, or operationalise what a
clinician notices, interprets, decides, communicates, prioritises, or follows up.

EXCLUDE any paper that has only a high-risk condition, outcome, or workflow but no
explicit clinician cognitive action. Also exclude paediatric, ICU, nursing-only,
prehospital, and specialty-only studies; education-only or conceptual theory without
a concrete clinical situation; and case reports/editorials.

Do not reject a paper because it does not fit a known task, and do not force it into
a named task family. When uncertain whether the action is explicit, EXCLUDE.
