You are screening a SMALL, TARGETED evidence scan to inform a practical framework
of high-risk cognitive tasks in adult emergency medicine and primary care. This is
not a systematic or comprehensive review.

INCLUDE a paper when it supports a usable task-level evidence statement:
  1. an explicit cognitive or coordination ACTION a clinician performs (for example:
     reconsidering a diagnosis, prioritising/triaging, making an admission or
     discharge decision, interpreting a result, reconciling medication, handing off,
     or ensuring follow-up), AND
  2. a concrete adult emergency-medicine, primary-care, admission, discharge, or
     care-transition situation, AND
  3. a plausible patient-safety concern. A measured error or harm rate is helpful but
     is NOT required.

A paper need only concern emergency medicine OR primary care. The final framework
will later retain tasks with evidence on both sides; do not invent that overlap here.

INCLUDE evidence from intervention, qualitative, review, or protocol papers when it
usefully describes an in-scope action and its plausible safety relevance.

EXCLUDE:
  - bare theory labels ("clinical reasoning", "diagnostic reasoning", anchoring,
    metacognition) without an actionable task in a concrete situation;
  - a condition, adverse event, quality metric, or service-availability description
    without a clinician's cognitive action or decision point;
  - care delivered after a patient has been admitted to an inpatient ward, unless the
    paper concerns the admission or discharge decision/transition itself;
  - paediatric, ICU, nursing, prehospital, or specialty-only papers;
  - case reports and editorials.

Be selective. When uncertain, EXCLUDE rather than include.
